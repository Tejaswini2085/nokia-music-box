Using your own MP3s:

To make your songs available to your Nokia over the internet, upload MP3 files into this website's music folder when you host it. Then add each filename to the list below in index.html. This static version does not upload files to the server by itself.

Free and legal music

Some tracks on these sites have download options. Check the licence and permission for each track.

  • Free Music Archive — independent music; licences vary.
  • Internet Archive: Audio — look for public-domain or openly licensed recordings.
  • Musopen — classical music; availability and licences vary.
  • ccMixter — Creative Commons music; check each track's terms.

How to use on your Nokia

  1. Open this website in Opera Mini.
  2. Choose a song and tap its download link.
  3. If asked where to save it, choose the memory card if available.
  4. Open Music or Gallery on the phone and play the MP3.

Tip: use simple filenames such as song01.mp3. The phone may not play every file if its encoding is unsupported.
